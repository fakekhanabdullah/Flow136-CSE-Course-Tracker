## 2. Description of Service

Flow136 is an open-source web application designed to assist students of BRAC University in simulating, planning, and tracking their academic progress towards the 136-credit Bachelor of Science in Computer Science and Engineering (B.Sc. in CSE) degree.

Key features include:
* Pre-loaded BRACU CSE 136-credit curriculum roadmap, prerequisites, and category credits.
* Term GPA and Cumulative CGPA performance simulation.
* Prerequisite validation engine.
* Optional Google Drive cloud backup synchronization via Google OAuth 2.0.
